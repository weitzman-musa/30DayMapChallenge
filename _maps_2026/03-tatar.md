Thailand is one of the world’s largest rice exporters, so naturally I love eating rice. My grandparents owned a rice store in Ubon Ratchathani province in the northeast (far right), which is also one of the country’s biggest rice producers during the main growing season.

The Northeast experiences extreme drought during the off-season, which drastically reduces production. By contrast, the Central region benefits from consistent irrigation from the Chao Phraya River, allowing for more stable year-round output

**Day 20 (Hexagons)**: New York, Day and Night compares estimated daytime and nighttime population density across the New York–New Jersey urban core. LandScan USA 2021 estimates are mapped as people per square kilometer of land in 500 m hexagons, revealing strong daytime concentrations in Midtown and Downtown Brooklyn and varied day–night patterns along the Hudson waterfront.

Both frames use the same hexagons, land-area denominator and color scale, switching every 800 ms in a 1.6-second loop. These are modeled baseline distributions, not observed real-time crowds or commuter routes.

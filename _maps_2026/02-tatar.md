Thailand is heavily car-dependent, while its existing railway network remains relatively limited, with many services still slow and outdated. For Day 2, I wanted to explore the country's railway network: Where do railways currently exist? What's under construction? And what is planned for the future?

I also brought in district-level population data to capture areas that have the greatest potential need for better railway connectivity. By combining existing and planned railway lines with population distribution, I hope to get a clearer picture of how Thailand's rail network serves its people today and where it could go next.

- [Railway: OverPass API](https://overpass-turbo.eu/) and [District](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-tha) shapefiles
- Population data from [National Statistical Office of Thailand](https://www.nso.go.th/nsoweb/nso/statistics_and_indicators?order=&search=%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B3%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%A0%E0%B8%AD&impt_side=&impt_branch=&impt_group=0&impt_subgroup=&year=&announcement_date=#gsc.tab=0)

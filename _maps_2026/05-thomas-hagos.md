**Two shores. One river. Different views.**

I paired six public places along the New York–New Jersey Lower Hudson waterfront with dated photographs and aerial maps. The three pairings compare opposite skylines, a skyline and sunset, and two frames on the George Washington Bridge.

[Explore the project](https://flex-commits.github.io/30-days-mapping/seeing-the-hudson/) · [Code, sources and reproduction](https://github.com/Flex-commits/30-days-mapping/tree/main/day05-seeing-hudson)

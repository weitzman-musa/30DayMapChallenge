If you have been to Thailand, getting woken up by the one annoyingly loud bird is a universal experience. That bird is known as the Asain Koel, a type of cuckoo bird. In its honor, I decided to map its sightings using data from the eBird app. Only birds that were taken a picture of were included.

On a side note, I read a book in middle school with the same title as this bird's name, as the plot involves aliens making every single woman in the town pregnant.

**Day 13 (Interactions)**: An origin–destination network of my own trips around Philadelphia from my [TripLibre](https://hhtdiary.org/) travel diary, Sept. 28 – Oct. 30, 2026. I expected the strongest link to be my commute. It isn't. Of **133 trips**, **41 began and ended on Penn's campus**, more than the **27** between campus and home. Campus works like a little city of its own: short walks from one building to the next, mostly on foot, with a median of about 430 m.

The map is laid out as one story sheet in Penn's colors.

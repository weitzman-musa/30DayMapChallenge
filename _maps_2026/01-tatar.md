For this year's challenge, I would like to create maps about Thailand! For day 1, I created an interactive map displaying all the malls in Bangkok. Malls are a prominent part of Thai people's lives, since they have everything from restaurants, food courts, grocery stores, gyms, movie theatres, bowling, indoor parks, and a load of other activities. And the most important part is that they have AC!

Using Google Earth to obtain accurate coordinates, I created a geojson file that contains the name, area, image, and coordinate of the largest malls in Bangkok. New malls are always being built and there are many smaller ones, so this dataset will be subject to change. I hope to use this dataset for future analysis on urban growth surrounding malls. Images were found across the internet.

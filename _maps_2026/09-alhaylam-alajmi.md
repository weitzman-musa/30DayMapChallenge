## Where the Desert Meets the City

**Riyadh, Saudi Arabia | Urban–Rural**

How does Riyadh's built environment vary across different levels of urban development?

This map explores spatial contrasts in Riyadh's urban fabric through building footprints and street network data. It combines a 60 × 60 km overview with three equally sized 2.5 × 2.5 km comparison windows.

| Urban condition | Building coverage |
|---|---:|
| Compact | 25.1% |
| Intermediate | 8.4% |
| Sparse | 1.0% |

Building coverage measures the proportion of each window occupied by mapped building footprints. The comparison illustrates differences in physical development intensity rather than official urban–rural classifications or historical urban expansion.

**Data:** Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints and © OpenStreetMap contributors.

**Tools:** Python, GeoPandas, Shapely, and Matplotlib.

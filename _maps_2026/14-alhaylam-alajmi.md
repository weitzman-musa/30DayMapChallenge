## La Carte Infinie — Paris

**Paris, France | Day 14 — A Borgesian Map**

*One city, infinite scales. / Une ville, mille échelles.*

How does our understanding of a city change as we move between geographic scales?

La Carte Infinie explores Paris through a multi-scale cartographic approach inspired by the concept of the Borgesian map. The project combines a detailed 2D atlas with an interactive 3D map to explore the relationship between the city, its neighborhoods, and individual urban features.

### Cartographic Approach

The atlas presents Paris at three connected scales:

1. **City scale:** The broader Paris study area.
2. **District scale:** A closer view of the Louvre neighborhood.
3. **Detail scale:** Individual buildings, streets, and urban features.

The connected views illustrate how geographic detail changes with scale and how different representations can reveal new perspectives on the same urban environment.

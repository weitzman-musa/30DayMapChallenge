**Day 19 (Senses: Smell)**: Where Smells Wander explores six adjoining SoHo blocks between Prince and Broome, from Mercer to Lafayette. A fluid outline moves through the spaces between buildings, revealing mapped smell evidence: amber for potential aroma sources from OpenStreetMap, violet for historical NYC 311 odor/fume reports, and neutral where nearby evidence is limited.

The 14-second animation presents a building-constrained spatial model. Points of interest are proxies and complaints are reports; colors do not represent measured odor concentration, health risk, real-time smell or validated physical diffusion.

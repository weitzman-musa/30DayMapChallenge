**Day 5 (Senses: Sight)**: Taking part in the NYC Ferry practicum and seeing the city from the water on ferry rides inspired this work. From the water, I notice how buildings overlap and how the waterfront reads as a continuous landscape.

**Seeing New York from the Water** borrows the slow unfolding of a handscroll. Three layers of building silhouettes slide at different speeds to create parallax, letting the scene gradually pass across the frame. I combined building footprints, height information, and landmark references to make an artistic view of New York's waterfront. The movement and silhouettes are arranged for this illustrated landscape. It is a schematic scene, rather than a reconstruction of an actual voyage or a precise simulation of visibility.

**Day 17 (Light & Dark)**: Where the Darkness Ends is an artistic night map of New York Harbor, combining documented navigation-light rhythms with illustrative lighthouse beams. Its 153 registered illuminated aids draw on the USCG Light List and NOAA Electronic Navigational Charts; they are not 153 lighthouses.

The 12-second loop uses artistic beams, glow and timing compression. It does not reconstruct synchronized light cycles or current operational conditions and is not for navigation.

![Where the Darkness Ends: navigation lights and illustrative lighthouse beams across New York Harbor]({{ '/assets/thumbnails/2026/17-mingcao.gif' | relative_url }})

**Sources:** [USCG Light Lists](https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/light-lists) and [NOAA ENC](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/charts/noaa-enc.html), using the retained research snapshot from November 17, 2026.

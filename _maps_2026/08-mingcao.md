**Day 8 (Utopia)**: Pushing a stroller has made me think about the pace of everyday streets. Inspired by Rittenhouse Square and West Walnut Open Streets, this map imagines a neighborhood where caregivers and babies can walk slowly, stop whenever they need to, play, and rest. The street network and park outline come from real geography; the activity scenes and street changes are imagined. It is a small vision of everyday life with more room to pause.
